## Trust by design

Human-centric AI starts with systems that explain decisions clearly and respect user context, goals, and limits.

## User control

Give people meaningful controls, including override options, review modes, and transparent confidence indicators.

## Explainability layers

Design outputs that show why a prediction happened, what inputs mattered, and when uncertainty is high.

## Ethical guardrails

Build policies for fairness testing, bias review, and escalation so trust is reinforced through consistent governance.

## Real-world applications

Trust-focused AI design improves adoption across critical workflows:

- **Healthcare tools –** surfacing interpretable risk signals so clinicians can validate and challenge recommendations.
- **Financial decisions –** providing reason codes for approvals and denials to support fairness and compliance.
- **HR screening systems –** exposing decision factors to reduce hidden bias and improve candidate transparency.
- **Customer-facing assistants –** signaling confidence and escalation options when responses may be uncertain.

Products that prioritize trust earn stronger user engagement and better long-term performance through quality feedback.

## Transparency in practice

Plain-language disclosures and clear system boundaries help users understand what AI can and cannot do reliably.

## Continuous feedback

Capture user corrections and dissatisfaction signals, then feed them into retraining and policy updates.

Human-centric Neural Networks succeed when transparency, control, and accountability are built in from day one.
