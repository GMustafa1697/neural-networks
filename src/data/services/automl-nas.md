## Headless Checkout Architecture

Deliver friction-free, sub-second checkout experiences built on Next.js Server Components. eComSmiths engineers bespoke cart and checkout flows that integrate seamlessly with Shopify Checkout Sheet Kit, Stripe Elements, and composable payment gateways to maximize checkout conversion.

### Overview

In modern e-commerce, every second of checkout delay costs revenue. Traditional monolithic checkouts suffer from script bloat, third-party tag contention, and clunky redirects that drive cart abandonment. eComSmiths designs and builds headless checkout architectures powered by Next.js App Router, streaming SSR, and edge execution.

We decouple the shopping and checkout experience, providing lightning-fast cart drawer interactions, instantaneous address autofill, optimistic cart updates, and robust tokenized payment processing. Whether you operate on Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, or custom composable backends, our Next.js checkout workflows ensure rock-solid PCI compliance while keeping your customers engaged without page reload friction.

At eComSmiths, we take an engineering-first approach to conversion rate optimization. By shifting API validation to Vercel Edge functions and caching inventory availability, our checkouts handle high-concurrency flash drops effortlessly without dropping orders or slowing down peak traffic.
