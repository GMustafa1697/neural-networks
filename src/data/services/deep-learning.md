## Headless Storefront Migration & Decoupling

Break free from rigid monolithic themes. eComSmiths migrates bloated Shopify Liquid, Magento, and WooCommerce stores to custom Next.js App Router frontends without SEO loss, product downtime, or order disruptions.

![service-details-hero-img](/images/opai-img-14.png)

### Overview

Legacy e-commerce platforms force brands to make a compromise: accept rigid templating and sluggish mobile load times, or face costly redesigns. By decoupling your frontend with Next.js, you retain your trusted backend commerce engine (Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, Commerce Layer) while unlocking total design freedom and sub-second page loads.

eComSmiths specializes in phased, zero-downtime headless migrations. We carefully audit your existing URLs, redirect structures, canonical tags, schema markup, and third-party apps to guarantee that your organic search equity is fully preserved.

Our migrations implement modern React Server Components, streaming SSR, and edge caching, replacing hundreds of obsolete Liquid template tags and unbundled jQuery plugins with a clean, maintainable TypeScript architecture.
