### Summary

We helped a financial institution unify risk signals across channels and shorten the time from alert to analyst review. The work focused on explainable scoring, workflow integration, and tuning thresholds to balance precision with operational load.

### Outcomes

- Fewer false positives on high-volume transaction streams
- Faster triage for the fraud operations team
- Clearer audit trail for model-assisted decisions

### What they said:

> "We finally have one place to see risk context and act on it. The team reduced noise in our alerts without losing coverage on the cases that matter."
