### Summary

We partnered with a regional health network to deploy a predictive model that flags high-risk patients earlier in the care journey. The program combined structured clinical data with operational signals to prioritize outreach and reduce avoidable admissions.

### Outcomes

- Earlier identification of cohorts at elevated risk
- Better alignment between care management teams and primary care
- Measurable reduction in manual chart review time

### What they said:

> "The model gave our care teams a shared view of risk and a clearer queue for intervention. We are seeing fewer surprises in the inpatient setting."
