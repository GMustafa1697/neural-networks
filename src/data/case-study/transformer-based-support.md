Transformer models can transform support when intent recognition, retrieval, and safe generation are wired to real policies—not generic chat. This program combined domain-tuned language models with a knowledge layer and human handoff so customers got fast, accurate answers without increasing risk.

### Client Overview

A global SaaS provider faced rising ticket volume after product expansion. Tier-1 teams were overloaded with repetitive questions, while complex issues waited too long for specialists. Leadership wanted automation that reduced load but preserved quality and compliance.

### Challenges

- Fragmented help content and overlapping intents  
- Multilingual demand with inconsistent macro quality  
- Strict requirements for citations and policy adherence  
- Need for seamless escalation to human agents  

### Strategy & Approach

#### 1. Knowledge & intent foundation

- Unified article and ticket taxonomy for retrieval  
- Intent and entity models trained on historical conversations  
- Guardrails for regulated topics and account-specific data  

#### 2. Transformer-assisted resolution

- Retrieval-augmented responses with source links  
- Suggested replies and next-best-action for agents  
- Continuous evaluation on golden sets and user feedback  

#### 3. Measurement & rollout

- A/B testing on deflection and CSAT by segment  
- Quality review workflows before full automation  

### Results

- **65%** reduction in avoidable tier-1 ticket volume  
- **3.2×** faster median resolution for eligible intents  
- Higher CSAT on assisted flows versus baseline macros  

### Key Takeaways

Transformers amplify support when retrieval, policy, and measurement are first-class. The win is fewer low-value tickets and faster paths for everything else.

### What they said:

> "Customers get accurate answers in one touch more often, and our agents spend time on problems that actually need a human."
